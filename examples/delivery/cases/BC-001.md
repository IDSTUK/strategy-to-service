# BC-001: Online meter readings

## The problem
Customers on metered tariffs phone to give a reading when their bill looks estimated. 4,100 calls a month (Aug 2026, contact centre system), average 6.5 minutes each.

## Objective served
OBJ-01 and, through call cost, OBJ-02.

## Options
| Option | What it is | Cost (range) | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | Calls continue, growing 5% a year with metering | £0 build, £148k/yr in calls | Rising cost |
| A: Web form | Reading form on the account page | £40k to £60k | Low take-up if hard to find |
| B: Form plus prompt | Form plus an email prompt when a bill would be estimated | £70k to £95k | Email consent coverage |

**Preferred:** B, because the prompt reaches customers before they call.

## Full cost (preferred option)
| Item | One-off | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| Build effort | £60k to £75k | |
| Business time (product owner, contact centre testers, training) | £12k | |
| Running cost | | £6k |
| Licences, AI and tooling | £3k | £2k |
| **Total** | £75k to £90k | £8k |

## Value case
| Level | Measure | Baseline | Target and date | Data source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome | Meter reading calls per month | 4,100 (Aug 2026, contact centre system, measured) | 2,000 by Mar 2027 | Contact centre system, call reason "meter reading" |
| Early signal | Share of readings submitted online | 0% (measured) | 40% by week 6 after release | Product analytics |
| Product events | reading_form_viewed, reading_submitted, prompt_email_clicked | n/a | n/a | Product analytics |

**Money conversion:** calls avoided x £3.00 per call (fully loaded, 6.5 minutes). Agreed with Leo Grant on 2026-09-08.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash benefit (agency overflow hours not bought) | £30k | £45k | £45k |
| Capacity benefit (not cash) | £25k | £30k | £30k |

**Counter-measure:** complaints about estimated bills must not rise above 210 a month (baseline 190).

**Stop criteria:** If online share has not reached 15% by week 6 after release, we re-plan the prompt or stop.
