# F-002: Reading measures and dashboard

Emits reading_form_viewed, reading_submitted and prompt_email_clicked, and shows on one dashboard: calls per month (from the contact centre system), online share, and complaints about estimated bills, each against baseline and target.

Includes the release switch that keeps 10% of customers on the old journey as a comparison group for six weeks.
